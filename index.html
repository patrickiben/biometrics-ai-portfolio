PK and clinical pharmacology

Residual carryover and the ICH M13A 5% pre-dose rule

Tools built: runs

Exploratory (synthetic)Subtraction: sensitivity analysis

How the ICH M13A 5% pre-dose rule performs when washout is borderline: consumer risk, power and exclusions for washouts of 2 to 10 mean half-lives.

At 5 mean half-lives the rule drops 34% of participants; consumer risk 0.051 (MCSE 0.005).

Within-sibling evaluation of the coding assist

Tools built

Synthetic, non-MedDRA data

With the neighborhood of terms given, does the coding assist pick the right member? An adaptation of the within-site permutation design from the AlphaGenome audit.

Within-set accuracy 0.909 (lab) and 0.861 (site) against a keyword rule's 0.970 and 0.819; both route to human coding.
