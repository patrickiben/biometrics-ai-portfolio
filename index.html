Biostatistics and trial-operations tools for consulting work

Runnable SAS and R templates, dashboards and checks for the work a biostatistics consultant does for a client across an early-phase trial, from the protocol's statistical sections to CSR text. Everything here runs from static files or in local R, so a client's data never has to leave your machine. The example studies (CP-101 to CP-108) are synthetic.
