eCRF pipeline apps in your browser

Four independent Python (Streamlit) apps for clinical eCRF extracts, each a four-tier chain with its own audit log: MedDRA-style auto-coding of adverse-event verbatims, PII/PHI flagging and redaction, participant safety and discontinuation risk scoring, and anomaly and drift detection. Labeled [SI] stand-ins run until trained runtimes are installed. They run in your browser (stlite); the demos use synthetic data and placeholder codes, not MedDRA.

Guardrails (apply throughout): operations only: no PHI and no participant-level data leave the client's systems; reported and regulated numbers come only from validated tools (Phoenix WinNonlin, Pinnacle 21, the EDC/CTMS), never from a language model; every deliverable is signed by a named statistician. These are working templates and tools to adapt and validate for each client study, not validated production systems.
