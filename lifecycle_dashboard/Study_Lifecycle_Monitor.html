How the operational risk score works (transparent, no black box) Each dimension is a plain weighted 0–100 score, scaled by how close the relevant gate is: Timeline = overdue milestones + days-to-DBL pressure · Data = open-query backlog + (1−clean%) + (1−SDV%), weighted by DBL proximity · TLF = (1−finalized/planned) weighted by reporting proximity · Resource = double-programming lag + open discrepancies. Green <33, Watch 33–66, At risk >66. No predictive model, no experimental method, just the numbers your CTMS/EDC/TLF tracker already hold.
