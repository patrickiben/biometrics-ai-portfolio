01 · R apps

R apps with Shinylive builds

The site's publishing workflow exports these three R Shiny apps with Shinylive, so R starts inside your browser and the console takes several seconds to appear. The first visit downloads about 55 MB, shared with the other two apps on this site; later visits reuse the browser cache. It works best in Chrome or Edge; Safari support may vary. Exported and checked in headless Chrome locally; Safari and Firefox not checked. A Run in browser link appears on a card here only once a dated first-visit check is recorded for that app; until then each card links its read-only snapshot, its guide and the command that runs it in local R.

02 · Python apps

Python in your browser (stlite)

Open an app to run it in your browser on bundled synthetic data (uploads are disabled in the public demo). The page is downloaded from GitHub Pages and jsDelivr, which see your IP address; the first visit downloads about 30 MB and Python starts in a few seconds. Not a validated or Part 11 system.

AI and automation

eCRF assist apps

Synthetic data

Four Python apps: coding QC assist, de-identification review assist, participant risk screening (rule-based default thresholds) and central statistical monitoring. The evaluation reports its own failures: on held-out synthetic text, the de-identification stand-in left 108 of 308 identifiers readable (the full model path: 91 of 308).

In your browser: GitHub Pages and jsDelivr see your IP address; about 30 MB on first visit; uploads disabled. Not a validated or Part 11 system.
