Biostatistics

The hands-on build: the artifacts a biostatistician actually uses. ADaM-based TLF pseudocode in both SAS and R, a narrative-interpretation drafter that never sources a number, the full Protocol→CSR deliverables pipeline, an end-to-end synthetic study sandbox, a worked bioequivalence study and the validation harness. These are templates and working tools to adapt and validate for each client study, not validated production systems.

Core deliverables & pipeline

TLF pseudocode libraries 221 + 221

ADaM-based Table/Listing/Figure pseudocode for every early-phase design (parallel, crossover, single-sequence, SAD/MAD), in both SAS and R (pharmaverse: dplyr + rtables + ggplot2 + emmeans). Geometric PK on the log scale, participants-not-rows, period denominators. The SAS programs have not been executed (no SAS license); the R programs parse but have not been run on data.

TLF interpretation drafter skeleton

A Quarto + on-prem small-model skeleton that auto-drafts TLF narrative text. The model never sources a number: deterministic facts feed a number-validated draft, behind a human gate. The self-test rejects hallucinated numbers. The model step is a stub; the number validator and its self-test run.
tlf_interpret

The complete Phase-1 clin-pharm deliverables pipeline (136 deliverables from protocol to CSR) as a register, a reference doc, and a visual map, with a worked PK example.

Study-lifecycle sandbox runs / verified

A public-calibrated synthetic early-phase study running all the tools end-to-end: 8 ADaM domains, a 1-compartment PK + NCA with a 2×2 bioequivalence ANOVA, and AE / vitals / labs / ECG summaries, with a Monte-Carlo calibration check.
sim_lifecycle

Worked bioequivalence study runs

One 2×2 crossover study taken from PowerTOST sample size and a blockrand schedule through an ICH E9(R1) estimand and SAP excerpt, admiral ADaM with a Pinnacle 21-layout spec and XPT files, PKNCA, the ICH M13A analysis, rtables/tern tables with RTF, a QC re-derivation by a second method, and CSR section 11-style text. One script (study/run_all.R) regenerates everything; CI reruns it. QC is a same-author re-derivation, not independent double programming.

eCRF pipeline apps in your browser

Four independent Python (Streamlit) apps for clinical eCRF extracts, each a four-tier chain with its own audit log: MedDRA-style auto-coding of adverse-event verbatims, PII/PHI flagging and redaction, participant safety and discontinuation risk scoring, and anomaly and drift detection. Labeled [SI] stand-ins run until trained runtimes are installed. They run in your browser (stlite), so nothing is sent to a server; the demos use synthetic data and 99-prefixed placeholder codes, not MedDRA, and the QC suite runs in CI.

Validation & QC

Trial-Management Validation Harness 8 of 10 gates green

Ten gates for a clinical-biometrics deliverable: reproducibility, PK and count conventions, data structure and integrity (custom checks, not Pinnacle 21 or CDISC CORE), SAS/R twin structural parity (SAS not executed), an adversarial QC panel (a manual record), reporting conventions, AI-use documentation (awaiting sign-off), package consistency, an independent recomputation of the sandbox results, and a seeded-defect test of what the gates catch. The checks run on this repo's own libraries and synthetic data and write an evidence ledger.

Biostatistics guardrails: reported/regulated numbers come only from validated tools (Phoenix WinNonlin, Pinnacle 21), never an LLM; "participant" not "subject" (CDISC USUBJID/SUBJID preserved); every AI draft is human-signed.
