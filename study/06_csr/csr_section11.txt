11.1 Data Sets Analyzed
Of the 40 participants randomized, all 40 received at least one dose and form the safety set. Participant SIMBE01-101-017 discontinued the study (withdrawal by participant). The PK concentration set includes 40 participants and the PK parameter set 40. Participant SIMBE01-101-029 vomited within 2 hours of a dose, so that period was excluded under the pre-specified rule. The bioequivalence set includes 38 participants (19 in each sequence), above the guideline minimum of 12 evaluable participants.

11.2 Demographic and Other Baseline Characteristics
Mean (SD) age in the safety set was 43.8 (7.5) years (range 32.0 to 59.0 years). Of the 40 participants, 21 were female (52.5%). Mean (SD) BMI was 25.2 (5.2) kg/m2. Table 14.1.2 gives the characteristics by sequence.

11.3 Measurements of Treatment Compliance
Each participant was scheduled to receive 2 single supervised oral doses. In total, 79 doses were given (40 Test and 39 Reference); the participant who withdrew did not receive the Period 2 dose.

11.4.1 Analysis of Pharmacokinetics and Bioequivalence
The adjusted geometric mean Cmax was 103.3 ng/mL for Test and 102.2 ng/mL for Reference. The ratio of geometric means (Test/Reference) was 101.07% with a 90% confidence interval of 98.89% to 103.30%. For AUC(0-t) the adjusted geometric means were 1204.9 and 1161.5 h*ng/mL, and the ratio was 103.74% (90% CI 98.38% to 109.39%). Both intervals lie within 80.00% to 125.00% (Table 14.4.3.1).
The within-participant CV was 5.6% for Cmax and 13.8% for AUC(0-t), below the 25% assumed for the sample size. The supportive AUC(0-inf) ratio was 105.10% (90% CI 99.24% to 111.30%). Median tmax was 1.05 hours for Test and 1.03 hours for Reference (Table 14.4.4.1).
In the ANOVA for ln(Cmax) the p-values were 0.8797 for sequence, 0.1657 for period and 0.4156 for formulation; for ln(AUC(0-t)) they were 0.5510, 0.7015 and 0.2501 (Table 14.4.3.2). As the SAP specifies, no test for carry-over was made; pre-dose concentrations were examined instead.

11.4.2.2 Handling of Dropouts or Missing Data
No values were imputed. One 12-hour sample was not collected and the profile was analyzed without it. Concentrations below the LLOQ of 0.50 ng/mL were set to zero and left out of the terminal-phase fit. One Period 2 pre-dose concentration was 2.5% of that period's Cmax, below the 5% limit, so the period was kept. AUC(0-t) covered less than 80% of AUC(0-inf) in 2 of 78 profiles (2.6%), within the 20% allowed before the study's validity needs discussion.

11.4.7 Conclusions
The 90% confidence intervals for the Test/Reference ratios of Cmax (98.89% to 103.30%) and AUC(0-t) (98.38% to 109.39%) both lie within 80.00% to 125.00%, so bioequivalence of the test and reference tablets is concluded for this synthetic study. The results were re-derived by a second method by the same author (37 of 37 QC checks passed); a second programmer did not re-derive them.
