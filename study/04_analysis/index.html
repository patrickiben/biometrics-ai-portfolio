NCA with PKNCA 0.12.1, configured per the SAP: actual times, linear-up/log-down AUC, conc.blq = list(first = "keep", middle = "keep", last = "keep") so BLQ counts as zero everywhere (ICH M13A), lambda z from at least 3 points after tmax. The bioequivalence model is the ICH M13A general linear model (sequence, participant within sequence, period, formulation). Tables use rtables 0.6.17 and tern 0.9.13; RTF comes from formatters::export_as_rtf() (formatters 0.5.13 with r2rtf 1.3.1).
