Monitoring, safety & coverage

SAS/R monitoring, coverage & continuity runnable templates

Deterministic, no-AI monitoring that detects, packages, and pages humans: freshness gate → checks → roll-up → digest → tier-2 alert → heartbeat. The coverage playbook started with "covering while on leave" (service account, named backup, dead-man's switch) and now handles five scenarios: planned leave, unplanned absence, role handoff or departure, a new owner taking over, and bus-factor review. The Continuity Toolkit turns a team's job inventory into a coverage matrix, a risk register, per-job checklists and a printable handoff packet, all in the browser.

Participant-safety dashboard (TRIALMON) R Shiny + multimodal

The safety-monitoring backbone made visible: the eDISH Hy's-Law screen, QTcF (ICH E14), the 3+3 DLT ladder, AE by SOC×grade, and a per-participant evidence packet. Now a runnable R Shiny app (click the eDISH scatter to open a packet), a visual guide (the AND-gate as a diagram + live screens), a shareable dashboard, and a build-it walkthrough, all from one shared R logic core. A screening lens, never a reported number; the medical monitor / SRC / DSMB decide.

Study setup, UAT & project stewardship

IRT / RTSM User Acceptance Testing R Shiny + multimodal

Run a UAT of the IRT/RTSM (randomization & supply) system before go-live, from the biostatistics lens, confirming the vendor build implements the protocol's randomization (allocation, stratification, blocking, concealment, dose-escalation logic), dispensing, resupply, and emergency unblinding. Delivered five ways from one shared R logic core: a visual guide (Mermaid diagrams + a live gate computed from the data), a runnable R Shiny app you edit and own, a shareable dashboard, a fillable Excel workbook (formula-driven go-live gate) for the eTMF, and a build-it walkthrough. Synthetic test data.

Hours budget reconciliation: "find hours" R Shiny + multimodal

When an out-of-scope deliverable suddenly needs doing, where do the hours come from? A planned-vs-actual-vs-forecast (EAC) effort reconciliation: net findable = (Σ planned + contingency) − Σ EAC, then a decision tree: reallocate under-run slack → contingency → defer → change order. Delivered five ways from one shared R logic core: a visual guide (decision-tree + source-map diagrams, a live roll-up), a runnable R Shiny app, a shareable dashboard, a fillable Excel workbook, and a build-it walkthrough. Hours/effort only, no dollars; synthetic.
