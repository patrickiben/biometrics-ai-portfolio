Monitoring and interim review

TRIALMON: safety review on synthetic data

A worked Safety Review Committee example for a first-in-human dose-escalation study: the screening rules, a console that applies them, and the packet a committee would read before deciding on the next dose.

Led: DSMB reports (Emmes) Supported: interim data review (Syneos Health) Tools built: this module (runs; tests pass) Gap: DMC charters, group-sequential interim design, BOIN or CRM design

Synthetic data only. Study CP-101 does not exist. TRIALMON flags positions for a medical monitor and a committee; it does not adjudicate DLTs or Hy's Law cases and makes no dose-escalation decision. It is not a validated system and produces no reported numbers.

Definitions used

  • Potential Hy's Law: ALT or AST at least 3x ULN, and total bilirubin above 2x ULN on or after that day within 30 days (an SAP convention). ALP is shown alongside; "ALP below 2x ULN" applies only as a labeled SAP option. Source: FDA, Drug-Induced Liver Injury: Premarketing Clinical Evaluation (July 2009).
  • Confirmation: any ALT or AST above 3x ULN calls for repeat ALT, AST, ALP and total bilirubin within 48 to 72 hours. Participant CP101-009 (ALT 4.2x, AST 3.6x, bilirubin 1.6x ULN) is flagged for it, not shown green.
  • QTcF: maximum post-baseline value above 450, 480 and 500 ms; increase from baseline above 30 and 60 ms (ICH E14 and its Q&As).
  • Lab grades: NCI CTCAE v6.0 by default or v5.0 per protocol, named in every footnote; creatinine on multiples of ULN and fold over baseline.
  • Dose escalation: a 3+3 tally as an illustrative rule reference only; BOIN is noted as the model-assisted alternative and not implemented. The committee decides.
  • Roll-up: worst-of at every level, so one RED is never averaged away.

What was executed

DataSynthetic, ADaM-shaped CSVs written from one seed: 18 participants in four dosed cohorts, with planted cases for each rule.
Rules and consoleOne R file of rules drives the console, the guide, the snapshot and the packet. Tested with testthat and shiny::testServer() on R 4.6.0: 155 expectations pass, including each rule at its exact threshold.
PagesRendered with Quarto 1.10.19 on 25 October 2026; checked in headless Chrome in light and dark at desktop and phone widths.
Not doneNo validated system, no independent programming, no review by a medical monitor. The CTCAE v6.0 cut points follow admiral 1.5.0's metadata and were not re-checked against the NCI workbook. Drafted with AI assistance; the author's sign-off has not been given yet.
