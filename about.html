About

About this toolkit

Worked examples, templates, apps and checks for the work a lead biostatistician does on an early-phase clinical trial, from sample size to clinical study report text. It is free to read, run and adapt; every dataset is synthetic.

01 · Contents

What is here

  • A worked study: one synthetic bioequivalence study taken through design, the SAP, ADaM, NCA and the bioequivalence analysis, QC and CSR text, with every program and output
  • The lifecycle by task: for each stage, what you can use here and what is not covered
  • Apps: a safety-review console, a UAT tracker, a study lifecycle monitor and eCRF assist apps, most of which run in the browser
  • Methods, libraries and checks: SAS and R TLF templates for five early-phase designs, method studies and a QC harness

02 · Conventions

What every page follows

Every dataset is synthetic; nothing here is real trial data, and no output here is a validated or regulatory deliverable. Each page says what was actually executed and what was not, in the words defined on Quality and AI use, and lists what it does not cover. In a real study, reported numbers come from validated systems and a named statistician signs every deliverable.

The source is on GitHub. Corrections and questions are welcome as issues there.
