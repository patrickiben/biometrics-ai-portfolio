About

Patrick Iben

Independent biostatistician: seven years of clinical trial work (Emmes, Syneos Health), including lead biostatistician on early-phase clinical pharmacology studies, shown here through worked examples and tools on synthetic data across the clinical trial lifecycle.

01 · Experience

Experience

  1. December 2021 to October 2026

    Biostatistician II

    Syneos Health · Remote

    • Served as lead biostatistician from December 2022 on early-phase clinical pharmacology studies, including first-in-human, SAD/MAD, crossover, bioequivalence and bioavailability designs.
    • Owned protocol statistical sections, randomization schedules, analysis plans and mock tables, listings and figures; created or reviewed CDISC analysis specifications.
    • Performed PK/PD analyses, reviewed outputs and contributed to clinical study reports; reviewed interim study data, annotated CRFs and database designs against analysis requirements.

  2. September 2019 to December 2021

    Associate Biostatistician

    Emmes · Rockville, MD

    • Led and supported federally sponsored Phase 1 and Phase 3 opioid-use-disorder trials; wrote protocol and analysis-plan sections, DSMB reports and final statistical reports.
    • Built dynamic study-progress reporting and SAS/R tools; harmonized multi-clinic EHR data and supported external research partners.

  3. August 2016 to July 2019

    Research Assistant and Teaching Assistant

    University of Colorado Anschutz

    • Harmonized hospital databases containing millions of records and hundreds of derived variables for pediatric nursing-cost research and reuse in later studies.
    • Advised doctoral nursing candidates on design and sample size; taught graduate biostatistics review sessions and office hours.
